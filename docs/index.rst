WiValdi Benchmark
=================

The WiValdi benchmark is being performed under the umbrella of the IEA Wind TCP Task 57 Joint Assessment of Models.

Intro
-----

The WiValdi benchmark is based on measurements collected at the DLR Wind Energy Research Farm
in Krummendeich, Germany.
It follows the collaborative benchmarking style used in prior JAM benchmark efforts, with staged
participation and shared reference data.

.. note::

   This site is a placeholder. Content will be added as the benchmark is defined.

How to Participate
------------------

- Review the page :doc:`description` for campaign context and motivation.
- Contact the benchmark leader and participate in working group meetings.
- Submit modeling results following guidelines given in the :doc:`submissions` page.
